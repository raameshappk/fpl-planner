What goes into it

Availability inputs this week

    From public team news on 2 and 3 November: Arsenal, Liverpool, City and Man Utd, Villa. Check the latest press conferences before acting.

    How the projection works

    1. Team strengthAttack and defence ratings fitted to this season's results, adjusted for opponent, weighted to recent games and blended with expected goals.
    2. MinutesSeason and last five matches, injury flags and rotation notes.
    3. PointsFPL's scoring rules applied to chances created, clean-sheet odds, bonus and defensive actions.
    4. PlanningOne optimiser picks squad, captain, transfers and chip timing across three gameweeks, counting a 4-point cost for each extra transfer.
    Treat the numbers as a ranking more than a forecast. The model has not been backtested yet, and it probably rates defenders too highly.
