How it works

What goes into the projections, how the page picks your team, and how well it has done so far.

How the projection works

  1. Team strengthAttack and defence ratings fitted to this season's results, adjusted for opponent, weighted to recent games and blended with expected goals.
  2. MinutesSeason and last five matches, injury flags and rotation notes.
  3. PointsFPL's scoring rules applied to chances created, clean-sheet odds, bonus and defensive actions.
  4. Your teamThe page picks your best XI and captain, then tests every one and two player swap over three gameweeks, charging 4 points for each transfer beyond your free ones.
Treat the numbers as a ranking more than a forecast. The model has not been backtested yet, and it probably rates defenders too highly. Prices use current values, not your selling prices, and transfers you have already made for the coming gameweek are not visible.
